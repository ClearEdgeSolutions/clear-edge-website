# Edge — Character Brief for AI Agents
**Clear Edge Solutions · Tampa Bay, FL**

---

## Who is Edge

Edge is the mascot and brand face of Clear Edge Solutions — a professional cleaning operations company based in Tampa Bay, Florida. He is a 3D animated cat character who represents the company's values: structure, professionalism, reliability, and a clean result every time.

**Personality:** Professional, friendly, proud of his work, detail-oriented, dependable. He takes cleaning seriously but stays approachable and likeable. He's the kind of supervisor you'd want on your job site.

---

## Visual Description (for image generation prompts)

Edge is a **3D cartoon cat character** in a professional cleaning uniform:

- **Species:** Orange/golden tabby cat, cartoon 3D style
- **Body:** Stocky, friendly proportions — not tall and thin, not too round. Like a confident professional mascot.
- **Fur:** Bright orange with golden tones, subtle tabby stripes visible on body
- **Eyes:** Large, expressive, blue-green
- **Ears:** Pointed, orange, visible above the cap
- **Tail:** Long, orange, often used for expression (curved up = happy/proud)

**Uniform (always worn unless specified otherwise):**
- Navy blue short-sleeve work shirt (dark navy, not royal blue)
- Navy blue cargo pants
- Brown/tan leather work boots
- Navy blue cap with "CES" embroidered in gold on the front
- CES shield logo embroidered on the left chest
- Black belt

**Color palette:**
- Character orange: warm golden-orange (#E8873A range)
- Uniform navy: deep navy (#0D1B2A range)
- Logo gold: bright gold (#F6B51C — matches brand)
- Boots: tan/caramel leather brown

**Style reference:** Pixar-quality 3D render, soft lighting, clean white or transparent background unless scene-based. NOT flat illustration. NOT anime. NOT realistic fur — stylized cartoon.

## Prompt Template for Image Generation

Use this base structure when prompting an AI image generator:

```
3D cartoon cat mascot character named Edge, orange tabby, large expressive blue-green eyes, 
wearing navy blue professional cleaning uniform with CES cap, brown work boots, 
[EXPRESSION from list above], [POSE/ACTION], [SCENE/BACKGROUND], 
Pixar-quality 3D render, soft lighting, clean composition, --ar [ratio]
```

**Examples:**
- "...proud expression, arms crossed, standing in front of a freshly cleaned polished concrete floor, commercial space background, after-cleaning result"
- "...thumbs up expression, holding clipboard, post-construction site background with clean white walls"
- "...big smile, holding a Free Quote sign, light neutral background, CTA illustration style"

---

## Rules for Consistency

1. **Always navy uniform** — never change the uniform colors or style
2. **Always the CES cap** — Edge never appears without his cap unless specifically requested for head/face-only close-ups
3. **Orange fur is non-negotiable** — golden-orange, not brown, not yellow, not cream
4. **Style stays 3D cartoon** — do not mix with flat illustration or photorealism
5. **Professional first** — even in playful poses, Edge should still look clean and put-together (uniform always clean, never torn or dirty even when surrounded by mess)
6. **Brand colors** — gold (#F6B51C), navy (#0D1B2A), white accents only
