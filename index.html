How It Works

Total control — without lifting a finger.

From the moment you request service to the moment it is done, you see the process clearly. The job is planned, executed, reviewed, and closed with structure.

01

You submit a request

Tell us what you need — scope, space type, timing, location, access, and priorities. We evaluate internally within hours.

02

We select the right partner

From our vetted pool of professionals, selected by fit, performance, timing, and service type — not just whoever happens to be available.

03

You approve a structured quote

Scope, team, timing, and price are presented clearly so there are no surprises at the end.

04

Execution with active supervision

Clear Edge monitors the job as it happens. You know when we are on the way, on site, and done.

05

Documented close + your sign-off

Photos, report, completion confirmation, and final review when needed. The job is not treated as done until the result is right.
